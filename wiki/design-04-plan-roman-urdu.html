Mera beta screen time pe bohat tantrum karta hai

Screen time khatam hone par gussa aana is umar mein aam baat hai.

  1. 1 Screen shuru karne se pehle batayein ke kitni der chalega.
  2. 2 Khatam hone se 5 minute pehle yaad dilayein.
  3. 3 Uske baad ek pasandeeda kaam tayyar rakhein, jaise khelna ya kahani.

Aapke bete ki umar kya hai, aur tantrum zyada tar kab hota hai?

Not a doctor or a lawyer. In an emergency, call 112.
