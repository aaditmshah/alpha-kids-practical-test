My 3-year-old refuses to sleep at bedtime

Bedtime battles at three are common, and they are often about wanting some control.

  1. 1 Start the same short routine at the same time every night.
  2. 2 Offer two small choices, like which pyjamas or which book.
  3. 3 Keep the goodnight brief and calm, and return quietly if they get up.

Try this for 3 nights, then tell me how bedtime went.

They also wake up crying at 2am most nights

Night waking at this age is common, and calm, boring responses help most.

  1. 1 Go in briefly, with the lights low and your voice quiet.
  2. 2 Use the same short phrase each time, like "It's sleep time, I'm close by."

If the night waking goes on for weeks or comes with loud snoring, mention it to a paediatrician.
