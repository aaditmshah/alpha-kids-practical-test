# Source: Alpha Kids Practical Test Brief

Back to: [Home](index.md) · [Sources](index-sources.md) · Interpreted in: [Requirements](requirements.md)

Received by the candidate from Alpha Kids as part of their interview process. Captured 2026-11-18. **Do not edit.**

> Build a small app in either Next.js or Node with a simple web UI. The user of the app, who is a parent, should be able to type a concern in English or Urdu. An LLM coach should reply with a short, structured, and safe plan along with a follow-up check-in, i.e. either a question or a time to come back. The replies should be brief and practical. The coach should not diagnose, should not give medical or legal instructions, and should clearly escalate if the parent describes an emergency, abuse, or self harm. A simple conversation history on the page is enough. No login or database is needed.
